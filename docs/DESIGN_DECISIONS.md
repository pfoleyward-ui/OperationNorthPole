# SantaBlizard — Approved visual direction

## Approved concept

Concept B: The Living Christmas Storybook, refined with a standing Santa portrait in the homepage hero. Aim for an old-fashioned Norman Rockwell-inspired Christmas wonderland without distracting, kitschy animations.

## Design principles

- Authentic SantaBlizard photographs are primary; generated mockups are references, not production page backgrounds.
- Warm ivory, antique gold, burgundy, deep evergreen, walnut, softly illuminated winter atmosphere.
- Vintage serif typography, tactile parchment, restrained ornamental borders, inviting copy.
- Keep navigation and booking pathways obvious; private operations dashboard should remain practical and modern.
- Mobile-first responsiveness and accessible contrast.

## Phase 1 implemented

- Responsive homepage navigation and hero with standing portrait.
- Five experience cards.
- Supporting About and Memories sections with original supplied photographs.
- Contact placeholder (not an active booking form).
